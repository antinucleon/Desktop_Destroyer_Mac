# Sound asset provenance

All bundled sound effects are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0).
They were converted to mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV and gain-adjusted for this app.

## Kenney — Impact Sounds

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

- `hammer.wav` — `impactGlass_heavy_000.ogg`
- `stamp.wav` — `impactSoft_heavy_000.ogg`
- `termites.wav` — `impactWood_light_004.ogg`, shortened and pitch-adjusted

## Kenney — Sci-fi Sounds

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds

- `flame.wav` — `thrusterFire_000.ogg`
- `phaser.wav` — `laserLarge_000.ogg`
- `phaser-hum.wav` — `forceField_000.ogg`

## OpenGameArt — Buzzsaw Sound Effect

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/buzzsaw-sound-effect

- `chainsaw.wav` — `saw.wav` by Spring Spring

## OpenGameArt — Basic Sound Effects

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/basic-sound-effects

- `machine.wav` — `gunshot.mp3` by n4, shortened and faded for rapid looping

## OpenGameArt — 40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx

- `color.wav` — `slime_03.ogg` by rubberduck
- `washing.wav` — `loop_water_02.ogg` by rubberduck
